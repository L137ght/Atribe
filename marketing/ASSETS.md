# Atribe Marketing Assets Tracker

This document outlines the specific marketing assets required for each phase of the Atribe launch campaign: "Power to the Tribe". 

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch / Teaser (Weeks -4 to -1)

**Goal:** Build waitlist, secure "Alpha" Creators, and onboard Beta Brands.

- [ ] **Teaser Video (Short-form)**: 15-30 second vertical video explaining "The Creator Economy is Broken. We're fixing it." for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
- [ ] **Waitlist Landing Page**: High-converting, single-page site with email capture and gamified referral system.
- [ ] **Creator Pitch Deck**: 5-7 slide presentation for direct outreach to secure "Founding Creators". Highlights value prop, revenue splits, and platform perks.
- [ ] **Brand Pitch Deck**: 5-7 slide presentation for Shopify merchants explaining the risk-free, performance-based model.
- [ ] **Email Sequences (Waitlist)**: 
  - Welcome email (Welcome to the Tribe)
  - Weekly update/hype emails (e.g., "Creator X just joined", "New feature sneak peek")
- [ ] **Social Media Graphics (Teaser)**: "Coming Soon" graphics, blurry UI teasers, and typography-focused value prop posts.

## Phase 2: Launch Day & Week ("The Tribe Unites")

**Goal:** Generate noise, activate the waitlist, and drive the first wave of transactions.

- [ ] **Hero Explainer Video**: 1-2 minute high-production, fast-paced video showcasing the 3-sided ecosystem (Fan -> Creator -> Brand).
- [ ] **Product Hunt Launch Assets**: 
  - Animated thumbnail logo.
  - 5-8 high-resolution product screenshots/mockups.
  - 1st-comment copy from the founder.
- [ ] **Shopify App Store Assets**: 
  - App icon/logo.
  - App Store promo video.
  - High-converting UI screenshots with descriptive text overlays.
- [ ] **Creator Media Kit**: Plug-and-play Notion doc/PDF for Founding Creators including:
  - Swipe copy for Tweets/Instagram Stories.
  - Standardized graphics ("Join my Tribe on Atribe").
  - Talking points for YouTube/Twitch shoutouts.
- [ ] **Launch Email Blast**: The official "We are live" announcement sent to the waitlist and beta users.
- [ ] **Updated Landing Page**: The full marketing site with 3D auto-flipping carousel for desktop and stacked cards for mobile.

## Phase 3: Post-Launch & Growth Loops (Weeks 2-8)

**Goal:** Sustain momentum, optimize viral loops, and build social proof.

- [ ] **"How It Works" Mini-Tutorials**: Series of 30-second screen-recording style videos:
  - "How to route a link and support your creator."
  - "How to share a link and earn support points."
  - "How to claim your creator reward."
- [ ] **Brand Case Study Template**: A standardized PDF/webpage template to showcase early success stories (e.g., "How Brand X got 500 new customers").
- [ ] **Creator Success Story Graphics**: Social media quote cards highlighting how much creators have earned or the unique rewards they've offered.
- [ ] **"Share to Earn" Promo Assets**: Graphics heavily promoting the feature where Supporters earn points by generating share links. 
- [ ] **Weekly Newsletter Template**: Standardized format for the community newsletter to keep the audience engaged with top creators, new brands, and platform updates.
